Our Story

North Star Bakery opened in 2015. It is a family-owned bakery. We wanted a warm place where neighbors can meet and enjoy fresh bread.

Our Sourcing

We use local flour and eggs from nearby farms. We believe good ingredients make good bread.

Our Staff

Our head baker, Marie, has baked bread for 20 years. Our pastry chef, Tom, designs all of our cakes.
